Debugging assistance
Caption/paragraph collision with shape-outside: I reported that body text was overlapping the figure caption at narrow widths. Claude initially misdiagnosed the fallback behavior of the shape's exclusion zone, then corrected itself once I provided a screenshot showing the actual overlap. The corrected explanation — that a CSS Shape's exclusion zone is defined by the shape function itself, not the shape unioned with the rest of the float's box, so surrounding content can flow into any part of the box the shape doesn't reach — was a hypothesis I tested by implementing and screenshotting the fix, not something I took on faith.
Fix approach for the collision: Claude suggested clear: inline-start on the trailing paragraph as a robust fix, independent of shape geometry. I implemented this, tested it across multiple viewport widths, and confirmed it resolved the issue via screenshots at 840px, 1087px, 1088px, and 1280px.
Responsive circle geometry: Claude suggested using a length-valued CSS custom property (--portrait-size: min(42vw, 20rem)) shared between width and the shape-outside: circle() calculation, to avoid the browser resolving a percentage-based circle radius against the shape's diagonal rather than its width. I tested this against the alternative (percentage-based circle) and confirmed the length-based approach produced correct, predictable results.
Breakpoint separation: After testing showed the shape treatment failing within the existing 52rem two-column breakpoint, I proposed moving the float/shape-outside rules to a separate 68rem breakpoint. Claude helped reason through the three-state responsive behavior (single column below 52rem, two-column without shape treatment 52–68rem, two-column with shape treatment above 68rem) and confirmed the approach via my own screenshot testing at the boundary.
